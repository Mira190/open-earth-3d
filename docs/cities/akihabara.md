# Akihabara

A map of Tokyo ([Tokyo as maps](tokyo-maps.md)): Electric Town from Manseibashi to Suehirocho,
and the Kanda heights to the west with Kanda Myojin and Nikolai-do. Frame: 1.8 × 1.48 km round
139.7705 E, 35.6995 N.

## Data

Overture Maps 2026-10-21.0 footprints, Project PLATEAU LOD2 heights (CC BY 4.0, via indigo-lab's
vector tiles), GSI DEM5A terrain and GSI aerial photographs, as for the other Tokyo maps.

## Dressing and landmarks (`cities/akihabara/city.py`)

- **Electric Town.** Every shop, office or unclassed building within 330 m of Chuo-dori's middle,
  6-80 m tall, gets signs on its street fronts (the edges that face a road within 7 m): a lit board
  over the shop front and one to three blade signs standing out from the wall, and one tall front
  in three a big print on its upper wall. The trades are those of the street (PC parts, games,
  anime, figures, cards, maid cafes, duty free); the signs are not real shops', and the prints are
  invented, with no characters, logos or art of any game or show.
- **Names on roofs.** Radio Kaikan, Don Quijote, GiGO and Yodobashi Akiba, their own words in open
  fonts on the roof edge that faces the nearest main road.
- **Kanda Myojin's Zuishinmon** (1975) on OpenStreetMap's footprint, its axis from the hall's
  footprint through the gate; the hall (1934) is given its vermilion.
- **Nikolai-do**: the nave held to 15 m, and the drum and green dome over it to about 35 m with
  its cross (Japanese Orthodox Church).

## Game

Chuo-dori, a collector on Junk Street, the survey lens, and a shrine attendant at Kanda Myojin.
