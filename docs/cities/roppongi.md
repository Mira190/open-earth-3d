# Roppongi

A map of Tokyo ([Tokyo as maps](tokyo-maps.md)): Roppongi from Nogizaka and the National Art Center
to Azabudai Hills, Roppongi Hills and Tokyo Midtown with the crossing between them. Frame:
1.8 × 1.6 km round 139.731 E, 35.6625 N.

## Data

Overture Maps 2026-10-21.0 footprints, Project PLATEAU LOD2 heights (CC BY 4.0, via indigo-lab's
vector tiles), GSI DEM5A terrain and GSI aerial photographs. 73% of the footprint area stands at a
PLATEAU-surveyed height (Mori Tower's 238 m, Midtown Tower's 248 m, Azabudai Hills Mori JP Tower's
325 m among them).

## Dressing and landmarks (`cities/roppongi/city.py`)

- **Maman** (Louise Bourgeois, 1999; Mori Building gives 9.27 m high and 10.0 m across) at
  OpenStreetMap's outline of it: a ribbed bronze body on eight jointed legs, and its sac of marble
  eggs.
- **The Mohri Garden's pond**, drawn at its own level (`pipeline/districtkit.py`) and water to the
  game (`game_ponds`).
- **The National Art Center, Tokyo** (Kisho Kurokawa, 2007): its front of glass louvers in a wave
  along the east face, swelling out in three crests and leaning out as it rises, about 21 m high, in
  front of PLATEAU's block of the building.
- **Keyakizaka's winter lights** (Roppongi Hills' illumination, November to December): a zelkova of
  lights every 9 m on both sides of Keyakizaka, its trunk and a dozen branches wound in blue or
  white LEDs.
- **The crossing's bars and clubs**: boards and blade signs in Japanese and English on the street
  fronts within 170 m of the Roppongi crossing (typical trades, no real shop's sign).

## Game

Roppongi on Foot starts at the crossing. A bartender sends you to a gallery guide under Maman, who sends
you down Keyakizaka; on the other line, a designer at Midtown sends you to a museum attendant at the
National Art Center. Station signs for Roppongi, Nogizaka, Roppongi-itchome and Azabu-juban.

## Not checked

- The glass wave's crests and lean against drawings; they are from photographs.
- The illumination is drawn every year round; the real one runs from November to Christmas.
