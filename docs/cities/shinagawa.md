# Shinagawa

A map of Tokyo ([Tokyo as maps](tokyo-maps.md)): Shinagawa Station and Takanawa Gateway between the
Takanawa hills and Konan's towers, and Kita-Shinagawa's old post town to the south. Frame:
1.8 × 1.8 km round 139.7395 E, 35.63 N.

## Data

Overture Maps 2026-10-21.0 footprints, Project PLATEAU LOD2 heights (CC BY 4.0, via indigo-lab's
vector tiles), GSI DEM5A terrain and GSI aerial photographs. 84% of the footprint area stands at a
PLATEAU-surveyed height.

## Landmarks (`cities/shinagawa/city.py`)

- **Takanawa Gateway Station** (Kengo Kuma, 2020): the roof of white membrane in folds about 7 m
  apart, rising and falling 2.6 m, highest over the concourse's middle, on timber-clad columns, with
  glass sides; on OpenStreetMap's footprint of the station, whose plain block is hidden. Lit after
  dark.
- **Shinagawa-shuku** (品川宿): the buildings within 12 m of the old Tokaido from the Yatsuyama
  bridge south get boards and blade signs (typical trades, no real shop's sign), and paper lanterns on
  posts line the road.

## Game

Shinagawa on Foot starts at the Konan exit. An office worker sends you up to a station guide at
Takanawa Gateway; on the other line, a sweet shop owner on the old Tokaido sends you along it and up to
the aquarium.

## Not checked

- The Takanawa Gateway City towers (THE LINKPILLAR, 2025) are in OpenStreetMap as one footprint each
  with their podium and have no surveyed height, so they stand at an estimated height, far lower than
  the real towers of about 160 m.
- The station roof's folds against drawings.
