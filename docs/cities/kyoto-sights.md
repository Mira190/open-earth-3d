# Kyoto sights: sources and figures

Scope: Kiyomizu-dera, Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka and the Yasaka pagoda, Fushimi Inari Taisha, Kyoto
Station Building, Detective Conan and anime pilgrimage spots, and Kyoto in video games.
Compiled 2026-11-04.

Legend: **[src]** is a figure taken from the cited page. **[?]** is uncertain: sources disagree,
the figure comes from a secondary or travel site, or the source page was not reachable. **[est]**
is an estimate from the OSM footprint or from photographs, and must be checked before it goes
into a model.

Coordinates are OSM centroids (WGS84), looked up through Nominatim
(`nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search`, 2026-11-04). Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The
Overpass API was unreachable from the build container that day; re-run an Overpass query by way
id to get the full footprints.

- JR West and Kyoto City announced on 2023-12-27 a new **bridge-level station building and free
  passage, west of the existing north-south passage**, to open in FY2031. It will link to a
  joint Japan Post / Kyoto Station Building Development building on the south side [?].

  https://www.city.kyoto.lg.jp/sogo/cmsfiles/contents/0000322/322027/shiryo.pdf

  work. The notice is dated 2026-10-01: https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/news/oozorahiroba-heisa/
- Station Piano WEST (west exit plaza) out of use from 2026-04-01; the east bicycle park closed
  2025-08-20 (official news list).
- The underground central exit closed 2022-12-25; the Nishinotoin exit closed 2023-05-07
  (ja.wikipedia).

Design ideas worth taking, in our own style:

1. **Landmarks as compass points.** The Yasaka pagoda, the Sanjunoto, Kyoto Tower and the
   station's long roof can each be seen from most streets. Keep their silhouettes readable from
   street level and through gaps in the roofs, and light them at night. Games do this rather than
   relying on a minimap.
2. **Compress climbs, keep proportions.** AC Shadows shortens the Kiyomizu approach. We stay 1:1,
   but can open the Sannenzaka steps and the Okunoin and Koyasu-to viewpoints as framed stops
   along the way.
3. **Dusk lanterns.** Higashiyama's lanes come alive at magic hour: warm 2,700 K andon and
   chochin against the blue sky, wet-looking setts, glowing noren. Shadows and Ishin both lean on
   it. Use our grade, not theirs.
4. **Density by layering.** Narrow lanes read as rich through crowds, shop props and signage at
   the base, while upper storeys and roofs stay simple. Put the detail budget in the first 3 m.
5. **Vermilion as a beacon.** In the Senbon Torii, the vermilion posts with black caps and their
   rhythmic shadow stripes are the whole experience. Light the gap spacing and the sun direction
   carefully (dappled light at noon, lamps at night), not the polygon count.
6. **Season as mood.** Autumn maples around the Kiyomizu stage (the Conan and Shadows look) and
   spring cherry blossom on Sannenzaka. A seasonal tree tint in `trees.mix` gives a lot of mood
   cheaply.
7. **Interiors.** Shadows was criticised for empty halls. At least the Hondo's inner sanctuary,
   with its hanging lanterns and dim glow, should read through the open front.
