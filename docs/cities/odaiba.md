# Odaiba

A map of Tokyo ([Tokyo as maps](tokyo-maps.md)): the islands of Odaiba and Ariake made in the bay,
from the seaside park and the Rainbow Bridge to Aomi and Ariake. Frame: 2.2 × 2.0 km round
139.771 E, 35.629 N.

## Data

Overture Maps 2026-10-21.0 footprints and water, Project PLATEAU LOD2 heights (CC BY 4.0, via
indigo-lab's vector tiles), GSI DEM5A terrain and GSI aerial photographs. 87% of the footprint area
stands at a PLATEAU-surveyed height.

## Landmarks (`cities/odaiba/city.py`)

- **The Rainbow Bridge** (1993; Metropolitan Expressway and the Port of Tokyo: 798 m, main span 570 m,
  towers 126 m above the sea) on OpenStreetMap's line of the expressway, from where it leaves Daiba
  for the sea: the anchorage, two white towers with three cross beams, the main cables and hangers,
  the truss between the upper and lower decks, and the upper deck rising from 30 m at the anchorage to
  50 m at mid-span. The approach viaducts east along Daiba come down from 30 m to 15 m over 1.2 km.
  Both are the game's road. The towers and cables are lit white after dark.
- **Fuji TV's sphere** (Kenzo Tange, 1996): a titanium ball 32 m across at about 104 m, between the
  towers of the FCG building.
- **The Statue of Liberty** (a replica of 2000, 12.25 m) on its pedestal at OpenStreetMap's
  position, facing the bridge.
- **The Yurikamome's guideway**: a concrete beam 9 m up on columns every 30 m along OpenStreetMap's
  line of the line, over the islands.
- **The canal bridges** (`districtkit.water_bridges`).

The giant robot statue at DiverCity is a licensed character and is left out.

## Game

Odaiba on Foot starts at Daiba Station. A studio tour guide sends you to the beach patrol, who sends you
to the Statue of Liberty and a photographer there, who sends you to look at the Rainbow Bridge; a
Yurikamome station hand sends you to Fuji TV's sphere.

## Not checked

- The towers' positions: they are set 114 m and 684 m along the expressway's line from where it
  leaves the land, not from a survey.
- The bridge's lower deck (the Yurikamome, the road and the footpaths) is drawn as the truss's
  floor, not as roads in the game.
