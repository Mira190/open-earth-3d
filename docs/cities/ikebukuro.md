# Ikebukuro

A map of Tokyo ([Tokyo as maps](tokyo-maps.md)): Ikebukuro's station and its two sides, Sunshine City
and Otome Road to the east, West Gate Park and the Metropolitan Theatre to the west. Frame: 1.8 × 1.6 km
round 139.715 E, 35.73 N. The ground is about 30-35 m above the sea.

## Data

Overture Maps 2026-10-21.0 footprints, Project PLATEAU LOD2 heights (CC BY 4.0, via indigo-lab's
vector tiles), GSI DEM5A terrain and GSI aerial photographs, as for the other Tokyo maps. 76% of the
footprint area stands at a PLATEAU-surveyed height; Sunshine 60's 240 m is PLATEAU's.

## Dressing and landmarks (`cities/ikebukuro/city.py`)

- **Sunshine 60-dori and Otome Road.** Shops, restaurants and unclassed buildings within 30 m of
  Sunshine 60-dori (from the east exit to Sunshine City), or within 14 m of OpenStreetMap's outline of
  Otome Road, get boards and blade signs on their street fronts: games, karaoke, ramen and arcades,
  and on Otome Road the books, goods and cafes of women's fandom. Typical trades, no real shop's sign
  and no character of any work.
- **Names on roofs.** Seibu, PARCO, Bic Camera and LABI, their own words in open fonts on the roof
  edge that faces the nearest main road (`districtkit.roof_names`).
- **GLOBAL RING** (Toshima City, 2019) over West Gate Park's open-air theatre: OpenStreetMap maps it
  as a plain ring of roof, which is hidden; it is drawn as five tilted bands of white steel within
  that ring's radii, each with a line of LED light along its foot, lit after dark, on eight raking
  legs. About 35 m across; its height, 8-11 m, is from photographs.

## Game

Ikebukuro on Foot starts at the east exit. A ramen chef sends you down Sunshine 60-dori to an aquarium
keeper at Sunshine City, who sends you to look up at the tower and on to a bookshop clerk on Otome
Road; on the west side, a street dancer meets you under the ring. Station signs for Ikebukuro,
Higashi-Ikebukuro and Toden-Zoshigaya.
